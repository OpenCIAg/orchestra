# Directive and service contract ledger

Reset for `22.4.0-rc.0` (onda A1): rows for the families, directives and services removed by [`docs/overhaul/DECISOES.md`](../overhaul/DECISOES.md) were dropped, links to deleted specs were unlinked, and the historical gate narrative of the compatibility period was retired (it remains in git history). The behavior evidence of each surviving family is rewritten by its wave-B executor.
